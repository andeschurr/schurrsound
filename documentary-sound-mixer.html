Documentary sound, small crews, real locations.

Guerilla crews, gorilla hours, and a plan about as straight as a banana. 🍌 Documentary is most of what I do and the work I like best.

Watch the showreelCheck availability

Drama gives you a second take. Documentary rarely does.

The interview happens once, the boat leaves when it leaves, and the man you flew in for will say it while a generator runs twenty metres away. Nobody is turning that generator off.

So the job is less about kit than deciding fast: what to fix, what to work around, what to tell the director now, and what to let go.

Lean and mean

  • The crewOften it is me, a camera operator and a director.
  • The rigRadios rigged so contributors forget them, a boom in my own hands when the shot allows.
  • The handoverTracks labelled and sent the day we wrap.
  • The bit people forgetRoom tone and wild tracks at every location, every time.
