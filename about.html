Ande Schurr, production sound mixer.

New Zealand born. Singapore based. Emmy-nominated for Lord of the Flies. Twenty years supporting directors, producers and editors wherever the story takes us.

Watch the showreelCredits

I do not fight the location.

Most of the hard part is not technical. It is deciding, fast, which battles are worth having with camera. A waterfall usually needs to be in the shot. A generator usually does not.

The rest is preparation nobody sees: frequency plans scanned before the day, rigs rebuilt when the costume changes, and wild tracks so post has a world to work with.

How I try to work

I once read that mission statement and it changed my life. It took the focus off the profit and onto the job, and it keeps me consistent whatever the climate.

That is the standard, and it does not move with the size of the job. A one-camera interview in a meeting room gets the same attention as four months of rainforest, because the person paying for the interview cares about their film exactly as much as the studio does about theirs.

I love the local work.

Between the long jobs I work in Singapore: interviews, brand films, documentary days. It keeps me in touch with the local crew and the local producers, who are themselves on the big jobs when they come.

The big jobs so far: Lord of the Flies for the BBC and Netflix, Monkey Man for Universal, Mr Midnight for Netflix and Hunt for the Wilderpeople for Taika Waititi, with documentary for the BBC, Netflix and Animal Planet.

The department

On anything larger than a documentary I run the department, with boom operators and utilities I have worked with for years, particularly in Indonesia and Malaysia.

On Lord of the Flies my lead boom operator was Adyt Santoso and my sound utility was Reza Feryanto, across four months of rainforest. Bigger series and features really sing when the whole department is in place.

Singapore is a deliberate base.

Every major production centre in Southeast Asia is one to three hours away, so a full drama package can be on set in Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Bangkok or Ho Chi Minh City the day after a call.

As a New Zealand citizen I work in New Zealand and Australia with no visa, no sponsorship and no cost to a production.
