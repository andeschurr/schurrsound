Why bother hiring a sound mixer based in Singapore?

Both countries have excellent sound departments. Hire locally and you will be well served. I am worth the call when:

  • The shoot crosses bordersCo-productions, two-region blocks, a story that moves between ANZ and Asia: one workflow, one set of file conventions, no handover halfway.
  • The location is hardRainforest, water, volcanic terrain, heat, remote. Most recently four months in the Malaysian rainforest.
  • Everyone local is bookedIt happens, especially in Auckland and on the Gold Coast in a busy season.

New Zealand specifically

I grew up there and worked there for years. Hunt for the Wilderpeople shot across Auckland and Mt Ruapehu; Oceans Apart for the BBC took me to White Island, Lake Taupo, Rotorua and Wellington.

The wind is what catches visiting crews: constant, with nothing to hide behind. Dealing with it is something you learn over years on location. Summer cicadas are the other, as broadband as any waterfall. How that gets handled.

Australia specifically

I have filmed across Perth, Adelaide, Sydney, Melbourne, Tasmania and Alice Springs. Most recently Adelaide, four years running, with LIV Golf.

Before you enquire

Do you need a work visa to shoot in Australia or New Zealand?

No. As a New Zealand citizen I have the automatic right to live and work in New Zealand, and under the Trans-Tasman Travel Arrangement the right to work in Australia without applying for a visa. There is no sponsorship, no permit application and no immigration paperwork for the production to complete or pay for.

Who covers travel and accommodation for an Australian or New Zealand shoot?

For both New Zealand and Australia I cover my own flights and accommodation to the base city, so the production is not paying to bring someone in from overseas. In practice the aim is that hiring me should not cost a production more than hiring locally.

Do you bring your own sound package to Australia and New Zealand?

The package is airline-checkable and travels. Whether it makes sense to bring it or to hire locally depends on the length of the block and the wireless frequency situation, since Australian and New Zealand permitted bands differ from Singapore's. It’s best decided when you book.
