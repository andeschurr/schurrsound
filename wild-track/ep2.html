I love the small jobs.

The Wild Track, issue 02. November 2026. A short letter and the map.

What is shooting, this week.

7 productions shooting or in prep across Australia, New Zealand and Southeast Asia. Checked every week now, with its public sources on every pin. Updated 19 November 2026.

Open the live map

    Dear friends,

    A confession: I love the small jobs.

    Most weeks I am on a one-day shoot somewhere in Singapore. An interview, a brand film, a documentary day. And the people beside me on those days are the same people I meet on the big ones.

    You are busy. You work internationally. You are doing co-productions. Then on Tuesday you are in a meeting room with one camera, and so am I.

    That is how this region works, and I would not change it. Big job or small, you get the same care.

    The map is how I keep up with you all. From this month it is checked every week, and every pin links to its public sources. If your production should be on it, reply and tell me.

    One new thing since July: I walked the stages at Iskandar Malaysia Studios in Johor and wrote down what I found, water tanks and all. Shooting in Johor.

    If you would like the map by email, there is now a short note on Mondays, sent only when something has changed. Sign up on the map. One click to leave.

    The coffee is still on me.

    Warmest regards,
    Ande Schurr
    Production sound mixer, Singapore. WhatsApp, ande@schurrsound.com
