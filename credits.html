Selected credits

Twenty years of location sound, on four continents.

I am a production sound mixer based in Singapore. I record dialogue and atmosphere on location for feature films, high-end television drama and documentary, working across Southeast Asia, Australia, New Zealand and beyond. There is more about how I got here if it helps.

Lord of the Flies

BBC / NetflixDrama series★ Emmy nomination

Director: Marc Munden · Producers: Joel Wilson, Devrell-Cameron · Line Producer: Magen Appathurai

Production sound mixer and sound department head on a four-month rainforest shoot, filmed almost entirely on location. Monsoon storms, running boys and dense jungle ambience, with a full surround wild track library recorded on location for post. Boom: Adyt Santoso. Utilities: Reza Feryanto.
