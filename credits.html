Selected credits

Twenty years of location sound, on four continents.

Ande Schurr is a production sound mixer based in Singapore. He records dialogue and atmosphere on location for feature films, high-end television drama and documentary, working across Southeast Asia, Australia, New Zealand and beyond.

2026

Lord of the Flies

BBC / NetflixDrama series★ Emmy nomination

Director: Marc Munden · Producers: Joel Wilson, Devrell-Cameron · Line Producer: Magen Appathurai

Filmed on location in Langkawi, Malaysia

Production sound mixer and sound department head on a four-month rainforest shoot, filmed almost entirely on location. Nominated for Outstanding Sound Mixing for a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie at the 2026 Primetime Emmy Awards. Monsoon storms, running boys and dense jungle ambience, with a full surround wild track library recorded on location for post. Boom: Adyt Santoso. Utilities: Reza Feryanto. 2024

Monkey Man

Universal PicturesFeature film

Director: Dev Patel · Producers: Basil Iwanyk, Samarth Sahni, Jomon Thomas

Filmed in Indonesia and Mumbai

Location sound on a fight-heavy feature shot largely in Batam, Indonesia, standing in for Mumbai. Practical locations, hand-to-hand action, crowded interiors and heavy set noise, with wireless coverage across a large ensemble.

2022

Mr Midnight: Beware the Monsters

NetflixDrama series

Director: Tony Tilse · Producers: Susan Oliver, Sean Masterson

Filmed in Singapore and Indonesia

Netflix's first Singapore original series. Young cast, practical locations across the city and a cross-border shoot into Indonesia, with the full drama package travelling between units.

2020

Somebody Feed Phil: Singapore

NetflixDocumentary series

Director: John Bedolis · Executive Producer: Rich Rosenthal

Filmed in Singapore

Run-and-gun documentary sound across hawker centres, restaurants and street locations. Wireless-first coverage in genuinely uncontrolled acoustic environments, working fast with a small travelling crew.

2019

Vet Gone Wild

Animal PlanetDocumentary series

Director: Mike Aho · Producer: Lyle Smith

Filmed in Costa Rica, Namibia, Vietnam, Thailand, Australia and Fiji

Six countries, one travelling sound package. Wildlife veterinary work in remote field conditions, where the kit has to clear customs, survive the climate and work first time with no second take available.

2018

Oceans Apart: Art and the Pacific

BBCDocumentary series

Director: Ben Harding · Presenter: Dr James Fox

Filmed in New Zealand: White Island, Lake Taupo, Wellington, Rotorua

Presenter-led arts documentary for BBC Four. Pieces to camera on an active volcano and on open water, alongside interviews and gallery recording where the room does most of the arguing.

2016
