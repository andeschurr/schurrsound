Showreel

Location sound, real conditions

People ask what the line at the top means. It means I do not fight the room. A waterfall, a market, cicadas, traffic, an air conditioner nobody can switch off: all of that is going to be there whether I like it or not. So I get the microphone close enough that the voice sits well above it, hold that position so the takes cut together, and record the place properly on its own so the edit can use it rather than fight it. The dialogue stays clean because the environment is working for the scene instead of on top of it.

Selected clips from recent international productions: clean dialogue, strong dynamic range, recorded where it happened.
