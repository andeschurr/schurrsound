Lord of the Flies: four months in Langkawi.

A BBC and Netflix limited series, directed by Marc Munden. Almost entirely on location: monsoon storms, running boys, dense jungle, and a full surround wild track library for post.

With actors

The story, the performance and the pictures matter more than my track. So I speak up quickly, between setups, and offer rather than demand.

  • Miking upIn the right sequence with costume and makeup, and the rigs rebuilt as often as the costume changes.
  • BlockingIn the director’s or the DOP’s ear when it is needed, and nowhere near it when it is not.
  • Young castsFamiliar ground: Lord of the Flies, Hunt for the Wilderpeople and Mr Midnight. A chaperone is present and I explain before miking.

With post

Post can only optimise what the actor gave you on the day. The job on set is to hand it something worth stretching.

  • Before the shootSync, naming, track layout, report format and one contact, agreed with your post team.
  • On the dayEveryone who speaks on their own track, named by person. Dual recording throughout.
  • TimecodeOne master, jammed to every camera and recorder at call and after lunch.
  • The worldRoom tone and wild tracks at every location, surround where the location deserves it.
  • At wrapOne merged sound report for all recorders, in the audio folder before the DIT leaves.

The department

On anything larger than a documentary I run the department, with boom operators and utilities I have worked with for years, particularly in Indonesia and Malaysia.

In Malaysia and Indonesia I hire crew locally. In New Zealand and Australia I am a local hire: my own travel and accommodation, and the full package travels with me.

Package
Sound Devices 833 with SL-2 expander, twelve Wisycom radio channels, DPA lavaliers, Sanken and Sennheiser booms, timecode to every camera, IFB to director and script, and comms. Airline-checkable.
Cover
Insured worldwide, equipment and liability, with certificates for production insurance.

Five New Zealand feature films

Each one I recorded as production sound mixer. Each plays its trailer here.

Vermilion 2018

Written and directed by Dorthe Scheffmann. Producers Michele Fantl, Nik Beachman and Paul Scantlebury. With Jennifer Ward-Lealand, Emily Campbell and Theresa Healey.

Born to Dance 2015

Directed by Tammy Davis, with choreography by Parris Goebel. Producers Jill Macnab, Leanne Saunders and Daniel Story. With Tia Maipi, Stan Walker and Kherington Payne.

Housebound 2014

Written and directed by Gerard Johnstone. Producer Luke Sharpe. With Morgana O’Reilly and Rima Te Wiata. A breakout hit from its world premiere at South by Southwest, with a US remake announced by New Line Cinema.

Orphans & Kingdoms 2014

Written and directed by Paolo Rotondo. Producer Fraser Brown. With Colin Moy, Calae Hignett-Morgan, Hanelle Harris and Jesse-James Rehu Pickery.

Fantail 2013

Directed by Curtis Vowell. Written by Sophie Henderson, who plays the lead. Producers Sarah Cook and Matt Noonan. With Stephen Lovatt, Jarod Rawiri and Jahalis Ngamotu.
