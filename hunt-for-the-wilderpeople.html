Piki Films, 2016  ·  Director Taika Waititi

Hunt for the Wilderpeople

Production sound mixer, New Zealand.

  • The New Zealand bush and the Mount Ruapehu foothills.
  • Chase sequences with Sam Neill and Julian Dennison.
  • Ten years old in 2026.

Trust the moment

Taika Waititi gives actors room to find things, and that kind of magic can’t be planned. So you watch, listen and stay ready for what happens when everyone’s attention is in the same place at the same time. Sam Neill, Julian Dennison, Rhys Darby, Rachel House and Cohen Holloway could each find something inside a take that wasn’t on the page. If the mics weren’t ready, the moment was gone.

Cold, wind and chases

We had the New Zealand bush, the cold and wind of the Mount Ruapehu foothills, and chase sequences with Sam Neill and Julian Dennison moving through rough country. That teaches you to hold the point of focus while everything around it is moving.

Ready before the camera rolls

The more I prepare before the camera rolls, the less I have to fight for during the take. I took that straight into Lord of the Flies.
